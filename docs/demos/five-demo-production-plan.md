Opening: "I want to see exactly where each payment contributes to this bond's price."

## 2. Financial-services due diligence

Viewer: an analyst reconciling a financial evidence packet.

Promise: identify a disagreement in dates, units, or reported figures without losing the supporting source.

Opening: "These documents describe the same business, but this number does not reconcile."

Use public or clearly labeled synthetic documents with an explicit expected discrepancy. Bonsai structures claims and proposes a review view. Jev classifies bounded evidence states such as supported, conflicting, and insufficient. Deterministic code checks arithmetic. Show the two cited passages, the unresolved question, and the review assignment. Do not claim a complete investment decision or a Jane Street endorsement.

## 3. Legal-services due diligence

Viewer: a lawyer or legal operations reviewer comparing a document packet.

Promise: expose a clause conflict or missing evidence and make it easy to inspect.

Opening: "The summary says this obligation is settled. The documents leave a question open."

Use public or synthetic agreements with visible dates, parties, and provenance. Local Bonsai proposes obligations and exceptions. Jev classifies bounded clause and evidence categories. Show the controlling passages together and retain unresolved issues for human review. The Latham public page can demonstrate source acquisition and firm profiling, but it is not evidence of private client matters or contractual obligations.
